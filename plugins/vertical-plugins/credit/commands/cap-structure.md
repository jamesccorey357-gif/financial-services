Load the `capital-structure` skill to lay out the debt stack by priority, the maturity schedule against cash and FCF, and (if asked or the credit is weak) recovery by tranche.

If an issuer is provided, use it. Otherwise ask the user for the issuer and the debt footnote or documents.
